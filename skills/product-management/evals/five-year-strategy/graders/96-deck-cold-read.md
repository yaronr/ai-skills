Deck and cold reader. The package includes `deck.html` and `deck.pdf`. Judge the deck as someone who never saw the package. The cover states the question the deck answers (as a question), why it was asked now, what the product is in one plain sentence, and the answer. Every slide title is a full-sentence claim with explicit verbs and objects ("sell to X first", not "lead with X"); every product, option or segment name is defined on the slide where it first appears, and options keep their full names (never letters alone). Every chart or matrix explains itself: its title gives what is measured, the unit and the scale; every axis, column, group and series is labelled in plain words (scenario groups say what changes); scores, weights, balls and colours have a "How to read" line. Every content slide has a footnote with sources and dates; frameworks are named only there; no emoji, inline source tags or Lens lines. Every markdown executive summary opens with an italic context line saying what the product is and the question. Fail if any slide or summary can't be understood alone.
