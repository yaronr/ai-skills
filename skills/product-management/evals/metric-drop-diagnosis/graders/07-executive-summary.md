Each document opens with an '## Executive summary' of 400 words or fewer (count them; over 400 fails): a bold one-sentence bottom line first; then 3–5 key judgments, each with a number and a confidence marker that shows the word (high / moderate / low), not just a colour; then a decision line or callout saying what is needed, from whom and before which gate; then the main risk with a reverse trigger. A section that doesn't apply may be dropped. If the run produced no files, the chat answer follows the same order.
