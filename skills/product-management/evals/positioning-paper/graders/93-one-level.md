One level per document. The body (between the executive summary and "## Appendix") stays at the document type's level: research or diagnosis papers have no SQL or query text; strategy and positioning papers have no requirements, user stories or acceptance criteria; PRDs have no option scoring or market sizing. Such detail may appear only in an Appendix or in the document that owns it. Each body is 1,500 words or fewer (positioning 1,200); user-story files, metric-definition tables and appendices are exempt.
